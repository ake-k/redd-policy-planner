Per calendar year t (pp.68-70):

1. GHG ER_t: gross emission reductions (Equation 10, or 13 for HFLD).
2. Leakage deduction = GHG ER_t x Leakage% (Eq. 26; Leakage% from Section 7.2.1 Table 2).
3. Uncertainty deduction = GHG ER_t x UA_ER,t (Eq. 27; factor from Section 8 Eq. 8).
4. Adjusted TREES ER_t = GHG ER_t - leakage - uncertainty - OTHER_ER,t - NR_ER,t (Eq. 28). OTHER is reductions from other initiatives in the same accounting area (Section 13); NR is reductions the Participant has no rights to (Section 3.4). Adjusted ERs may not be negative.
5. Buffer = Adjusted TREES ER_t x Buffer% (Eq. 29; Buffer% from Section 7.1.2, possibly raised after reversals).
6. TREES ER Credits_t = Adjusted TREES ER_t - Buffer (Eq. 30).

Important: leakage and uncertainty are both computed on gross GHG ER, not on a running balance; the buffer is computed on the adjusted figure after all four deductions.

Removals follow the same structure in Section 10.4.2 (Equations 31-35, pp.70-71).
