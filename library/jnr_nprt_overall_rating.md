Overall rating (Table 7) = PG + PDS + CR + FR + PL + NR, rounded up to the nearest whole percentage (2.3.1, p.16). Each category total is floored at zero even if its formula goes negative (2.1.2(1), p.5).

- Minimum: "The minimum risk rating shall be 10" (2.3.2, p.17).
- Fail: a rating greater than 60 means the program fails the whole risk analysis and cannot be credited until risks are reduced below that threshold (2.3.3, p.17).
- Buffer: the rating converts one-to-one to a percentage (35 -> 35%) applied to the verified net GHG benefit; buffer credits go to the jurisdictional pooled buffer account (2.3.4-2.3.5, p.17).

Insurance and replacement guarantees (2.1.2(5), pp.5-6):
- If a risk factor or whole category is fully covered by a non-VCS mechanism such as insurance, it may be scored zero, with documentation of coverage and beneficiaries.
- If a government or third party guarantees replacement of credits after a reversal, the rating applies only to the share not covered. Worked example: 100,000 tCO2e, 30% guaranteed, 20% rating -> 100,000 x 0.7 x 0.2 = 14,000 buffer credits. The guarantee must be reviewed by the JNR expert panel and Verra at initial validation.
