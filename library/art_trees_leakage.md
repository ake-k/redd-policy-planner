Leakage arises where a Participant uses a subnational crediting level, so emissions can shift outside the accounting area. TREES uses standardized deductions rather than measured leakage; they cover both activity-shifting and market leakage (7.2-7.2.1, p.56).

Table 2, based on share of national forest area included in TREES (p.57):
- High: below 25% -> 20% deduction
- Medium: 25-60% -> 10%
- Low: 60-90% -> 5%
- No leakage: above 90% -> 0%

The chosen percentage is "Leakage%" in Equation 26 (reductions) and Equation 31 (removals), applied to gross GHG ER or removals (p.56, pp.68-71). Note that national-scale accounting is defined as covering at least 90% of forest areas (3.1.1 fn.2, p.24), which matches the zero-leakage class.
