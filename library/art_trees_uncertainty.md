TREES adjusts reductions and removals for estimated uncertainty to limit over-crediting (p.58).

- Uncertainty is the half-width of the 90% confidence interval as a percent of estimated emissions; sampling error must be included. Model and allometric errors are excluded because they are treated as consistent between reference and crediting periods.
- Activity data and emission factors are both assessed, separately for reductions and removals, and propagated with Monte Carlo simulation (90% CI, n = 10,000; bootstrapping allowed if the distribution is unknown). Simulated values replace arithmetic values in Section 10.
- Adjustment factor (Equation 8): UA_ER,t = 0.524417 x (90% CI_ER,t / 1.645006); Equation 9 is the same for removals (pp.58-59).
- Deduction = gross ER x UA (Equation 27).
- At the end of a crediting period the Participant may recompute uncertainty on summed gross results; if lower than the annual values, extra TREES Credits are issued, and these also owe a buffer contribution (p.58, fn.22).
