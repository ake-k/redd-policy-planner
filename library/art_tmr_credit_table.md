The TREES Monitoring Report (TMR) template (Word document, no fixed page numbers) asks for one column per calendar year in this row order:

1. TREES Crediting Level (tCO2e)
2. GHG Emissions (tCO2e)
3. Emission Reductions: unadjusted, and from Monte Carlo simulations
4. Leakage: deduction % and TREES leakage deduction for ER
5. Uncertainty: adjustment factor and TREES uncertainty deduction for ER
6. Double Counting: ERs from other programs, and other ERs to which the Participant does not have rights
7. Adjusted TREES Emission Reductions
8. Buffer Pool: buffer contribution % and buffer pool contribution for ER
9. Total TREES Emission Reduction Credits

This mirrors TREES 3.0 Equations 26-30. A parallel table exists for the HFLD crediting approach, and the TREES 3.0 Calculation Template (or equivalent workbook) must be submitted alongside.

Related TMR instructions: the reversal risk rating table takes the Reversal Risk Rating Tool value each year, and "If the Participant had a reversal in the five years prior to the reporting year, please enter 30% as the total risk." Other projects/programs with verified ERRs or results-based payments must be disclosed in the double-counting table.
