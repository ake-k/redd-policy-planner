section: "4.2 Calculating a TREES Crediting Level for HFLD Participants"

High forest, low deforestation (HFLD) is an optional crediting-level approach (4.2, pp.34-37).

- Eligibility: HFLD Score = Forest Cover Score + Deforestation Rate Score, where FCS = (forest cover % - 50)/100 and DRS = 0.5 - annual deforestation rate %. The score must be 0.5 or higher in every year of the reference period; status holds for the five-year crediting period (4.2.1, pp.34-35).
- Crediting level: HFLD CL = TREES Crediting Level + (average HFLD Score x 0.05% of standing forest carbon stock, above- and below-ground tree biomass only) (Equation 5, p.36). The Participant must show this level is conservative against drivers in its REDD+ Implementation Plan.
- If annual emissions exceed the regular TREES Crediting Level, a deduction applies per Table 1 (none up to 15% above; rising in steps; no credits above 75%) (pp.36-37).
- HFLD credits are labeled as such in the ART Registry (p.34).
