Assesses the risk that funding not arriving on time undermines the program. The proponent supplies at least five years of forward financial projections covering program development, administration, emission-reducing activities and MRV, plus a forecast of all funding sources marking which are secured (p.12).

Breakeven is the year cumulative cash flow turns positive and stays positive. Percentage secured = secured funding and revenue divided by total cash outflow up to breakeven. Options contracts do not count as secured (pp.12-13).

Table 4 (p.13):
- a) Default: 6
- b) -2: breakeven five years or less from the analysis, or commercially viable emission-reducing activities provide at least 60% of required funding until breakeven
- c) -2: at least 40% of funding needed before breakeven is secured
- d) -1: a domestic voluntary or compliance market exists or is being established that will create demand for the program's credits
- e) -1: public-private REDD+ or sustainable landscape partnerships established

FR = a + b + c + d + e, not less than zero (p.14).
