Leakage is the net change in emissions outside the program boundary attributable to program activities (p.44).

Program rules (pp.44-46):
- Consider activity-shifting, market and ecological leakage; the VT0004 JNR Leakage Tool applies (3.16.1, 3.16.6).
- International leakage is not deducted, but national programs must try to mitigate it (3.16.2, 3.16.8).
- Positive leakage cannot be counted (3.16.5).
- Leakage from forested to non-forested wetlands (incl. peat) must be identified, mitigated and accounted for (3.16.7).
- Subnational programs mitigate and quantify significant leakage elsewhere in the country (3.16.9). If the neighbor is itself a registered jurisdictional program, each jurisdiction is responsible only for emissions inside its own boundary. If the neighbor has no monitoring or registration, use a leakage belt, VT0004, and/or monitoring of activity shifting within the jurisdiction (e.g., deforestation shifting to degradation) (3.16.10).
- Resulting leakage is subtracted from total jurisdictional reductions for the monitoring period (3.16.10, p.46).

Nested projects follow the VCS Standard and their methodology for leakage (3.16.11-3.16.12, p.46).
