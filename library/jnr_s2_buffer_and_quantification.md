Scenario 2 lets credits go to both the jurisdiction and nested projects/programs (2a), or only to nested activities (2b) (2.1, p.7).

Non-permanence (3.17, pp.47-48):
- Programs, including nested activities, need a minimum longevity of 40 years.
- The proponent prepares a non-permanence risk report under the JNR Non-Permanence Risk Tool; buffer credits are deposited in the single jurisdictional pooled buffer account and "are not VCUs and cannot be traded."
- A proponent may voluntarily contribute extra buffer credits, deducted after the tool-determined amount.
- Nested projects and lower-level programs also deposit into the jurisdictional pooled buffer account; pre-existing project buffers transfer after the transition period.

Quantification (3.18, pp.48-50):
- Net benefit = FREL emissions minus program emissions minus leakage.
- Jurisdiction credits = net reductions minus jurisdictional buffer, minus reductions issued (or issuable) to nested projects/programs including their buffers, minus credits for the same reductions under other GHG programs (3.18.6, p.49).
- Jurisdiction buffer = JNR risk rating x reductions issuable to the jurisdictional program only (3.18.7, p.49).
- Nested project buffer = AFOLU risk rating x carbon stock change only (3.18.11, p.50).
