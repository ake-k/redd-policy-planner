Assesses how carbon rights and revenue use could create disincentives or perverse incentives that lead to reversals, e.g., agents who reduce emissions but are not rewarded, or revenues not reinvested (p.10).

Table 3 (p.11):
- a) Laws/policies establishing clear, undisputable carbon rights not yet enacted -> 4; enacted -> 1
- b) +1 if carbon rights are tied to land tenure or access/use rights and less than 90% of the jurisdiction is free of overlapping rights or disputes
- c) Mitigation -1: statutory rights to lands, territories and resources established, or a funded process is under way
- d) Mitigation -1: mechanisms exist to resolve carbon-rights disputes
- e) Share of government carbon revenues used for non-REDD+ purposes: more than 40% -> 4; between 20% and up to 40% -> 2; less than 20% -> 1
- f) Mitigation -1: revenue-use policies developed with best-practice stakeholder involvement (e.g., REDD+ SES) and transparent expenditure accounting

CR = a + b + c + d + e + f, not less than 0.
