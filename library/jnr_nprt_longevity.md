Program longevity is the period over which the proponent keeps the legal right to control and operate program activities. "Program longevity shall be demonstrated for a minimum of 40 years." The whole longevity must be covered by public management, financial and monitoring plans (p.14).

Table 5 decision steps (p.14):
- Q1: Legally binding agreement covering at least 100 years from the start date -> PL = 0. Otherwise go to Q2.
- Q2: Longevity under 40 years -> the program fails the risk assessment.
- Q3: No management, financial and monitoring plan for the entire longevity -> fail.
- Q4: Legal agreement, policy or requirement to continue implementation?
  - Without: PL = 25 - (longevity / 5)
  - With: PL = 25 - (longevity / 4)

PL is not less than zero (p.15). Version 4.1 added longevity as a risk factor and set the 40-year minimum (Appendix 1, p.18).
