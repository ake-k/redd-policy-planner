Covers general political risk, rule of law and governance (not forest governance specifically) (p.6).

Governance score = mean of all six World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators averaged over the most recent five years of data (p.6).

Table 1 scoring (p.7):
- a) score < -0.9 -> 8; >= -0.9 and < -0.7 -> 6; >= -0.7 and < -0.4 -> 4; >= -0.4 and < 0 -> 2; "greater than 0" -> 1 (a score of exactly 0 is not explicitly assigned).
- b) +2 if the jurisdiction is subnational and the national government has no documented policy or public support for crediting or paying the subnational program. Set to zero for national programs or subnational programs coordinated by the national government.
- c) Mitigation -1: program structured to continue regardless of changes in government (e.g., independent of the elected government and/or protected by law).
- d) Mitigation -2: REDD+ readiness activities targeting governance with improved structures adopted (or, subnationally, governance shown to be better than the national rating).

PG = a + b + c + d, not less than zero.
