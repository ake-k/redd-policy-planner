Nesting (3.1, p.24): ART does not credit projects or smaller activities directly. It leaves it to each Participant to decide whether and how such activities are nested into its program.

Deduction for other programs (13.1, p.83):
- Verified ERRs from other projects or programs in the accounting area for the same calendar year, whether led by the Participant or others, are deducted from TREES issuance (Equations 28 and 33). This covers other GHG crediting programs, bilateral or multilateral agreements and results-based payments.
- The deduction uses verified volumes and includes any ERRs that could ever be issued for that year, which may include another program's buffer credits if they can later be returned and sold.
- Deductions are like-for-like (reduction credits against TREES reductions; removals against removals). Without a type distinction, they come off TREES emission reductions.
- Separately, ERRs the Participant lacks rights to (e.g., project areas without verified credits yet) must also be deducted (Section 3.4.1).

Double use and double claiming are handled through registry tracking, terms of use, and host-country authorizations with corresponding adjustments (13.2-13.3, pp.84-85).
