Required by TREES 3.0 Section 7.1.1; calculated for each reporting year and used as the buffer contribution (p.3).

Total risk rating = 5% assumed starting risk + the Participant's scores below (p.3).

Governance risk (p.3):
- REDD+ program governance (program or framework mandated by law, policy or binding instrument): No 2% / Yes 0%
- Country governance (five-year average WGI across all six indicators, on the percentile scale): below 45 -> 3%; 45 to below 55 -> 1%; 55 or above -> 0%

Program continuity risk (pp.3-4):
- Experience/capacity (prior results-based payments or climate finance participation before ART): none 3%; under 10 years 1%; 10+ years 0%
- Inclusivity (formal multi-stakeholder committee providing ongoing input, incl. IPLCs): No 2% / Yes 0%

Vulnerability to disturbances (p.4):
- Disturbance impact (share of forest in accounting area hit by fire, weather, flood, pests etc. over last five years, cumulative): above 1% -> 8%; 0.5-1% -> 5%; below 0.5% -> 0%
- Mitigation plans (robust prevention/response system): No 2% / Yes 0%

Range: 5% minimum to 25% maximum (5+2+3+3+2+8+2), consistent with the 25% "highest Risk Rating possible" in TREES 3.0 Section 7.1.3.
