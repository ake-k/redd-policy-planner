Under Scenario 1 the jurisdictional proponent (usually a government) develops and registers a forest reference emission level (FREL) for all or part of its territory and allocates it to nested REDD+ projects and lower-level jurisdictional programs as their baselines or FRELs (2.1, p.7).

Who receives credits:
- "Carbon accounting and crediting only occur for the nested REDD+ projects and lower-level jurisdictional programs" (p.7).
- The government may monitor to gather data for FREL reassessment but cannot seek VCU issuance (Box 1, p.8).
- Because nothing is issued to the higher-level jurisdiction, there are no authority/rights requirements for the jurisdictional FREL (3.6.1, p.14) and no other-GHG-program requirements (3.7.1, p.15).
- Nested projects follow VCS Standard ownership rules; lower-level programs follow Scenario 2 or 3 rights rules (3.6.2-3.6.3, p.15).

Buffer: nested projects use the AFOLU Non-Permanence Risk Tool and the AFOLU pooled buffer account; nested lower-level jurisdictional programs use the JNR tool and the jurisdictional pooled buffer account (2.2.1-2.2.2, p.8).
