Assesses reversal risk from natural disturbances (fire, pests, disease, extreme weather, geologic events), combined. The assessment area is the whole country or the broadest relevant eco-region(s) containing the jurisdiction, using historic data. Significance is the share of forest carbon stock (or forest area) affected (p.15).

Matrix (pp.15-16). Likelihood columns: every 1 to <10 yrs | 10 to <25 | 25 to <50 | 50 to <100 | once per 100+ yrs or not applicable.
- Catastrophic (>30%): Fail | 30 | 20 | 10 | 0
- Devastating (>20 to 30%): 30 | 20 | 15 | 5 | 0
- Massive (>15 to 20%): 20 | 15 | 10 | 3 | 0
- Major (>10 to 15%): 15 | 10 | 5 | 2 | 0
- Minor (>5 to 10%): 10 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 0
- Insignificant (5% or less): 0 in all columns

Mitigation (p.16): where measures to significantly mitigate major natural risks (those affecting 10% or more of forest carbon stocks or area) are in place and shown effective, subtract 20% of the initial score. NR = initial score + mitigation.
