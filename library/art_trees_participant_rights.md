Who is credited: Participants are national governments or subnational governments at most one level below national (subnational allowed through 31 December 2035 as an interim step). Accounting areas may not overlap. ART does not credit projects directly; a Participant may appoint an Agent (3.1-3.1.1, p.24).

Issuance: after verification and ART approval, credits are issued and, once the activation fee is paid, become active in the Participant's ART Registry account for transfer or retirement (2.1-2.2, pp.18-20).

Rights (3.4.1, p.29):
- The Participant must show, under existing legal frameworks, its rights to ERRs from the accounting area for each year claimed, and how competing claims (private parties, Indigenous Peoples, local communities, Afro-descendant Peoples, subnational entities) are resolved.
- Where other rights holders exist, transfer agreements must be evidenced and conform to TREES Safeguards.
- "TREES Credits will only be issued for the number of ERRs for which the Validation and Verification Body has verified that the Participant can demonstrate rights."
- ERRs without demonstrated rights are set aside (the NR term in Equation 28) and may be issued later in the same or next crediting period once rights are verified.
