Assesses whether the program design tackles the core agents and causes of deforestation (and degradation, where relevant) and limits long-term reversal risk. Because strategies are hard to compare objectively, the tool starts from a default and allows mitigations (pp.8-9). Drivers are classed as commodity drivers (products sold to markets) or subsistence drivers (household or local needs).

Table 2 (pp.9-10):
- a) Default rating: 10
- b) -3: production of significant commodity drivers is maintained (or the program does not affect commodity drivers); must not incentivize illegal commodities
- c) -3: measures address subsistence drivers and support most of the agents involved (or no effect on subsistence drivers)
- d) -2: program integrated into a government-led low-emission rural development or green economy plan
- e) -1: strategy developed in consultation with representative agents of deforestation
- f) -1: national government has received or is receiving REDD+ readiness funding from bilateral/multilateral donors

PDS = a + b + c + d + e + f. The general floor of zero for each category applies (2.1.2(1), p.5). Mitigations should be applied consistently with VT0004 JNR Leakage Tool where used (p.9).
