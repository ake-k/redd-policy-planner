VT0007 (UDef-A) generates and selects risk maps for allocating unplanned deforestation activity data to project baselines, including from jurisdictional FRELs. It aims for unbiased allocation, good identification of low-risk areas, minimal data needs and simplicity (2, p.4).

It standardizes three steps (p.4):
1. Build a benchmark jurisdictional map of unplanned deforestation risk.
2. Compare it with alternative risk maps to pick the best one.
3. Use the chosen map to allocate proportional shares of the jurisdictional activity-data baseline (VMD0055) or jurisdictional FREL (JNR) to projects or programs.

Who produces maps: "VT0007 is intended for official use by service providers contracted to act on Verra's behalf." Activity data allocated by Verra take precedence over other stakeholders' use of the tool (p.4).

Applies to (4, p.6): a standalone avoided unplanned deforestation project under VMD0055 seeking allocated jurisdictional activity data, or a higher-level jurisdiction allocating its FREL to projects or lower-level jurisdictions. A Python implementation (UDef-ARP) is referenced (5, p.6).
